# 🎃 TRICK OR BEAM — How to Play

**You are PIP**, a little mint-green blob in a cardboard robot costume.
Sneaky aliens called Zorblings are beaming up your friends — sneak through
Gumdrop Hollow and save everybody before midnight!

## The basics
- **Move** — Touch the LEFT side of the screen. A joystick pops up!
  - Push it a *little* = **SNEAK** (quiet and slow) 🤫
  - Push it *all the way* = **RUN** (fast but noisy!) 🏃
- **Green cones** on the ground are what aliens can see. Stay out of them!
- **"?"** means an alien is getting suspicious. **"!"** means RUN — it's
  chasing you!

## Your three big buttons (right side)
- **HIDE** — tap near leaf piles, bushes, trash cans, boxes, or porches to
  dive in. Hold it near a glowing **Jelly Jar** for 2 seconds to pop it and
  free a friend!
- **THROW** — hold, drag to aim, let go! Aliens LOVE candy and will waddle
  over to eat it. Great for sneaking past guards.
- **POWER** — your costume's special trick (freezing flashlight, ghost float,
  pumpkin roll, dino stomp…). Each rescued friend shares their costume!

## Sneaky tricks
- **Blend in**: stand still next to decorations that match your costume
  (pumpkin + jack-o'-lanterns, ghost + sheets on a line…). You'll shimmer
  when it works ✨
- **Noise rings**: running, crunchy leaves, and trash cans make rings that
  aliens hear. Sneak past Sniffers — they follow footprints!
- **Caught?** No big deal! You beam back to the last lamp post and drop half
  your candy (it's still there — go pick it up!). No game over, ever.
- **Clock**: each level has a Halloween clock. Slow is fine — you just earn
  fewer stars.
- After the last rescue — **RUN FOR THE EXIT!** 🚀

## Win stars
⭐ Rescue every friend · ⭐ Never get caught · ⭐ Find all 3 Golden Candy Corns

*Tip for grown-ups: keyboard works too — WASD/arrows, Shift to run,
E = action, Q = power, click = throw candy.*
