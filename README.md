# 🎃 TRICK OR BEAM

**A cozy kid-friendly Halloween stealth adventure.** You are **Pip**, a little mint-green blob in a cardboard robot costume. Goofy one-eyed aliens called the Zorblings are beaming up your friends — sneak through Gumdrop Hollow, free every friend, and escape before midnight!

## Play it

**https://borktaco.github.io/trick-or-beam/** (enable GitHub Pages on the `main` branch to activate)

No install, no build step — just open `index.html` in a browser. Best on iPad in landscape (add to Home Screen for fullscreen), also works on iPhone and desktop.

## How to play

- **Move** — touch the left side; a joystick appears. Push a little to **sneak** (quiet), push far to **run** (fast but noisy!)
- **Green cones** are what aliens see. `?` = suspicious, `!` = run, it's chasing you!
- **HIDE** — tap near leaf piles, bushes, trash cans, boxes, porches. Hold it near a glowing **Jelly Jar** for 2 seconds to free a friend.
- **THROW** — hold, drag to aim, let go. Aliens LOVE candy and will waddle over to eat it.
- **POWER** — your costume's special trick. Each rescued friend shares their costume!
- **Blend in** — stand still next to decorations matching your costume to shimmer into camouflage.
- Caught? No big deal — you beam back to the last lamp post. No game over, ever.

⭐ Earn stars: rescue every friend · never get caught · find all 3 Golden Candy Corns.

Keyboard: WASD/arrows, Shift = run, E = action, Q = power, click = throw candy.

## Contents

- `index.html` + `game.js` — the entire game (engine, 10 levels, story, shop, music). Split into two files only because GitHub's API rejects single uploads over 128KB; zero build step, just open `index.html`.
- `assets/` — title key art + 13 story illustrations (AI-generated), 180×180 home-screen icon
- `manifest.webmanifest` — fullscreen/landscape web-app manifest
- `HOW_TO_PLAY.md` — kid-friendly how-to card
- `CHOICES.md` — every autonomous build choice + verification notes

10 levels from Pumpkin Patch Lane (7:00 PM) to the Mothership (midnight), 8 unlockable costume powers, 7 alien types, Gourdley's shop, Treat Book, and 8 achievements.
